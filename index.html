ƒlatcam Support

Film looks on a flat capture, for iPhone

Getting started

Swipe left or right on the viewfinder, or tap the mode bar, to switch between PRO, NIGHT, PHOTO and VIDEO. Tap the looks button (three circles) to pick a look and set its strength. Tap the settings button (sliders) for exposure, profile, format, aspect, hand and grid.

Importing your own looks

Save a .cube LUT file to the Files app, then open Settings and tap “+ Import look”. Your own looks are listed first. To delete one, press and hold the LOOK setting while it is selected.

Camera Control and the Lock Screen

On iPhone 16 and 17, go to Settings → Camera → Camera Control and choose ƒlatcam. Light-press and slide to change zoom, exposure, look or strength; press to take the photo.

RAW

Set FORMAT to RAW+J to save a RAW file alongside the JPEG as one photo in your library.
