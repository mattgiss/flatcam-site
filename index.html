Getting started

ƒlatcam opens simple: a shutter, a recipe, the flash and your lenses. Tap the round recipe badge to pick a look, then take the photo. When you want every control, tap “ƒlatcam” at the top and choose “Switch to ƒlatcam Pro” for the dials, modes (photo, night, video) and settings. Pro’s settings have a switch to go back.

Importing your own looks

Save a .cube LUT file to the Files app, then in ƒlatcam Pro open Settings and tap “+ Import look”. Your own looks are listed first. To delete one, press and hold the LOOK setting while it is selected.

Camera Control and the Lock Screen

On iPhone 16 and 17, go to Settings → Camera → Camera Control and choose ƒlatcam. Light-press and slide to change zoom, exposure, look or strength; press to take the photo.

RAW

Set FORMAT to RAW+J to save a RAW file alongside the JPEG as one photo in your library.
