ƒlatcam Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

ƒlatcam is a camera app. It does not collect, store or share any personal data, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Camera

ƒlatcam uses the camera only to show the viewfinder and take the photos you ask it to take. Camera images are processed on your device and never leave it.

Microphone

ƒlatcam uses the microphone only while you record a video, to add sound to that video. Audio is processed on your device and never leaves it.

Photos

ƒlatcam asks for permission to add photos to your library so it can save the pictures you take. It does not read, browse or upload your existing photos.

Looks you import

If you import a .cube look file, ƒlatcam copies it into the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the look or the app removes it.

Settings

Your choices (look, strength, exposure, aspect, hand, grid, mode and video settings) are stored on your device only.

Network

ƒlatcam does not connect to the internet.

Children

ƒlatcam collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
